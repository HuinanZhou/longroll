Support

LongRoll turns a scrolling screen into one long screenshot. Everything happens on your iPhone.

How to make a long screenshot

  1. Open LongRoll and tap Start Long Capture.
  2. Tap Start Broadcast.
  3. Switch to the app you want to capture and scroll down slowly.
  4. Tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen and stop the broadcast.
  5. Tap the notification to open LongRoll. Review the image, then save or share it.

Tips

  • Scroll at a steady speed. Short pauses are fine.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so other notifications do not appear in the capture.
  • Nothing is saved to Photos until you tap Save to Photos.

帮助与支持

LongRoll 把滚动的屏幕截成一张长截图。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

怎样截长图

  1. 打开 LongRoll，轻点"开始长截图"。
  2. 轻点"开始直播"。
  3. 切换到要截图的 App，慢慢向下滚动。
  4. 轻点屏幕顶部的红色录制标识，停止直播。
  5. 轻点通知回到 LongRoll。查看长截图，然后存储或共享。

小提示

  • 滚动速度保持平稳。中间停一下没关系。
  • 打开"勿扰模式"，避免其他通知被截进去。
  • 只有你轻点"存储到照片"后，才会存储到照片。
