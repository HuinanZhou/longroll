Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

LongRoll does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Screen content

When you start a capture, iOS asks you to start a screen broadcast. LongRoll's broadcast extension reads frames of your screen while the broadcast runs. These frames:

  • stay on your device, in the app's private storage;
  • are used only to build your long screenshot;
  • are deleted after the long screenshot is made, or when you delete it.

LongRoll never uploads screen content. The app has no network features.

Photos

LongRoll asks for add-only access to your photo library. It uses this only to save a long screenshot when you tap Save to Photos. It cannot read your existing photos.

Notifications

LongRoll uses local notifications to tell you that a long screenshot is ready. These notifications are created on your device. No push service is used.

Analytics, ads and tracking

LongRoll has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party SDKs.

Children

LongRoll does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

LongRoll 不收集、不上传、不共享、不出售任何个人数据。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

屏幕内容

开始长截图时，iOS 会请你开始屏幕直播。直播进行时，LongRoll 的直播扩展会读取屏幕画面。这些画面：

  • 只保存在你的设备上，位于 App 的私有存储空间中；
  • 只用于生成你的长截图；
  • 在长截图生成后，或在你删除长截图时，会被删除。

LongRoll 从不上传屏幕内容。App 没有任何联网功能。

照片

LongRoll 会请求"仅添加"照片的权限。只有在你轻点"存储到照片"时，才会用它存储长截图。LongRoll 无法读取你已有的照片。

通知

LongRoll 使用本地通知告诉你长截图已准备好。通知在你的设备上生成，不使用推送服务。

数据分析、广告和追踪

LongRoll 没有数据分析，没有广告，也不追踪你。LongRoll 不使用第三方 SDK。

儿童

LongRoll 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。
